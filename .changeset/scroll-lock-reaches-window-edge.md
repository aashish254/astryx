[fix] `useScrollLock`: modal overlays reach the window edge under a classic (space-taking) scrollbar. With a Drawer, fullscreen Dialog, MobileNav, BottomSheet, or Lightbox open on Windows, Linux, or a Mac set to always show scroll bars, a blank strip of page background the width of the scrollbar showed along the window's right edge: the surface stopped short of it and a full-bleed divider inside appeared to end early. The lock held that gutter closed with `scrollbar-gutter: stable` so the page would not reflow, but a reserved gutter sits outside the layout viewport that top-layer surfaces and their backdrops are sized against, and nothing paints into it. The lock now lets the viewport take the scrollbar's space and holds the page itself: the pinned body is inset from the right edge by exactly the width the viewport gained, measured rather than assumed, so in-flow content keeps its box and no inset is applied for overlay scrollbars or on pages that reserve the gutter themselves. That width is published on the root as `--astryx-scroll-lock-gutter` for the duration of the lock; the toast viewport reads it so a toast on screen does not move when a modal opens, and `position: fixed` or top-layer chrome aligned to the viewport's right edge can add `var(--astryx-scroll-lock-gutter, 0px)` to its right inset to do the same. MobileNav now locks through this shared hook instead of clipping the document itself, and both MobileNav and Lab's Drawer keep the lock through their slide-out so the returning scrollbar cannot nudge the panel mid-exit.
