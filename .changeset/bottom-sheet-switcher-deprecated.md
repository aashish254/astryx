[feat] `BottomSheetSwitcher` and the `sheetId` prop of `BottomSheet` are deprecated. The replacement is already in this release: write each step of a multi-step flow as its own standalone `BottomSheet` with `isOpen`, driven by one state value that names the open step; closing the top sheet and opening the next in the same update runs the same replace handoff the switcher ran (entering step above, a taller retained step aligns down and fades after both motions, one steady scrim, focus into the new step and back to the flow's opener on close). Migration, per flow: (1) replace the `activeSheet` / `onActiveSheetChange` pair with one `step` state value; (2) give each step `isOpen={step === 'id'}` and `onOpenChange={isOpen => !isOpen && setStep(current => (current === 'id' ? null : current))}` in place of `sheetId="id"` (the guard keeps a step that is being replaced from clearing the step the person just chose); (3) remove the `BottomSheetSwitcher` wrapper, moving its `hasScrim` onto every step; (4) keep the step buttons as they were (`setStep('next')`). Flows that keep using the switcher keep working: a switcher flow used on its own behaves as released, and when it is stacked with other sheets the stacking behaviour of this release applies to it as one level (it recedes and is blocked when covered, shares the one scrim, stands down from dismissal while covered, and returns focus by the stacking rules). Its declarations and docs are marked `@deprecated` and its docs, templates and stories show only the replacement, until a future minor release removes it. Non-modal flows (`hasScrim={false}`) migrate the same way: non-modal sheets replace each other too.
